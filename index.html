Arrow Media Player — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Developer: Tinesa

Contact: arrowmediaplayer@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Arrow Media Player (“Arrow”, “the app”) is a media player designed to play video, audio and image files, along with associated subtitle files, selected by the user on compatible Android and Google TV devices.

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow accesses and handles information when you use the app.

2. Information We Collect

Arrow operates locally on your device. The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number or email address to use its media playback features.

Arrow does not collect or transmit personal information, advertising identifiers, diagnostic information or media file contents to the developer.

3. Files and Device Storage

To provide its core functionality, Arrow may access media files and folders that you select or authorize it to access, including files on connected external storage devices.

This access is used to browse and play supported media, read associated subtitles, and provide playback-related features such as resuming playback.

Media files are processed on your device. Arrow does not upload your personal media library or file contents to the developer.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Arrow may maintain local application preferences and playback-related information, such as playback position or recently accessed media, where these features are enabled.

This information is used to provide the app’s functionality and is stored locally. Arrow does not transmit it to the developer.

You can remove locally stored application information by using the device’s applicable app settings or clearing the app’s data. Uninstalling the app may also remove its locally stored application data.

5. Sharing of Information

Arrow does not sell personal information. The developer does not receive or share users’ personal information or media files through the app.

6. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

Arrow’s core functionality is local media playback. The app does not integrate advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party data-collection services, and does not send user information or media files to online services.

7. Data Security and Retention

Arrow is designed to process media locally and to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information.

Playback-related information and app preferences retained on the device remain subject to the device’s storage, security and backup settings. The developer does not maintain a server-side database of users’ media files or playback histories.

8. Children’s Privacy

Arrow is a general-purpose media player and does not require users to submit personal information to use its core playback functionality. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of devices and any media accessed through them.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Arrow’s functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy policy URL provided in the app’s Google Play listing.
